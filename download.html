On a Mac or Linux? The new apps are for Windows first. Mac and Linux versions come later.

On Windows

Three steps and you're in.

  1. Download

    Press the button of the app you want. The installer is one small file.

  2. Open it

    The apps are new and not code-signed yet, so Windows may warn you. Press More info, then Run anyway. No admin rights are needed.

  3. Start making

    The app is on your Start menu and desktop. Each app keeps its own settings.

Good to know.

Native and fast

The apps are built from the ground up as native Windows programs. They open in a moment and use little memory.

Yours only

No account, no tracking. Your files and settings stay on your PC. Cloud AI is used only if you add your own key.

Use them together

Install one app or all of them. They sit side by side and do not change each other's settings.

Easy to remove

Open Settings, Apps, find the app and press Uninstall. Your settings in AppData stay, in case you come back.

Version 2.0.0

Newest
  • Aether is now a native app. It starts in a moment, uses far less memory and is a much smaller download. Everything you had is still there.

  • No admin rights. Each app installs for you only, with a Start menu and desktop shortcut and an entry in Settings, Apps to remove it.
