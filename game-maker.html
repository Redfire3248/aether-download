2D and 3D

Paint levels from tiles, or bring in 3D models (.glb, .gltf, .fbx, .obj) with their animations, and pose your own on a timeline.

Play inside the app

Press Play to test as a PC or a phone, then Stop and keep building. Scripts in Aether Script or JavaScript.

Multiplayer

Host over Wi-Fi so phones and PCs play together, or go online from anywhere with Firebase, with accounts and an admin panel.

Press Play

Test it the moment you make it.

A Studio-style editor: a ribbon of tools, the Explorer on the left, Properties on the right, and your game in the middle.

  • Scripts from a gallery: platformer and top-down players, enemies, coins, doors, waves and chat.
  • PC and phone apps from one game: a Windows .exe, an Android .apk and a web game together.
  • Publish to the web: your game on GitHub Pages, with a link anyone can open.
