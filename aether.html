AI that stays yours

Ask, edit, apply.

The AI reads the files you point it at with @file, then shows each change as a card you can apply or check first.

  • On your PC: Ollama, LM Studio or any OpenAI-style server. Nothing leaves your computer.
  • With your own key: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek and xAI.
  • Autocomplete as you type, and commands for docs, tests and commit messages.

Find the bug

Stop on the line. See everything.

Press F9 for a breakpoint, F6 to start. Step over, into and out, and hover any name to see its value.

  • Variables, Watch and Call stack in one panel, with a console that works out expressions while paused.
  • Breakpoints with conditions, and stop on every error if you like.
  • Live Run runs your file again each time you change it.
